# Medu: Cairo Edition (prototype)

A clickable, installable preview of Medu: audio plays you walk inside historic places. Episode 1 is Sultan Hassan in Cairo; the other monuments on the map are coming soon.

**Open it:** https://kiko9111.github.io/medu-prototype/

This build is the **super app prototype** (Today, Map, Lens, Souk, Passport). The plain street-test edition is at https://kiko9111.github.io/medu-prototype/?edition=preview

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, then tap **Install the app** on the first screen (or Chrome menu, then *Install app*).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

After the first visit it works offline.

## Status of the content

This is a design prototype, not the product. Narration texts are drafts that have not been checked against
sources (UNVERIFIED). Dates and coordinates are approximate and must be verified. Floor plans are schematic sketches,
not surveyed plans. The narration is pre-recorded with an open-source neural voice; a slide whose text changed after recording falls back to the phone's text-to-speech.
