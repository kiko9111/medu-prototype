Try this

  1. Begin, then start Episode 1 at Sultan Hassan.
  2. Choose "I'm somewhere else" to hear it as one continuous story.
  3. The prologue and the first two scenes are free; then a mock "preview edition" screen asks to continue.
  4. Swipe the photos, drag the Then & Now handle, tap the key plan.
  5. The other monuments on the map are coming soon.

Arrow keys swipe, Esc returns to the map. On a phone, open the same link in Chrome for the full-screen feel.
