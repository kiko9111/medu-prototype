Try this

  1. Begin, pick a walk at the top of the map, and tap a numbered stop.
  2. Watch the year roll back to when it was built.
  3. Swipe the photos; tap the key plan to enlarge it.
  4. Drag the Then & Now slider at Bab Zuweila.
  5. Return to 2026, and finish every stop on a walk.

Arrow keys swipe, Esc returns to the map. On a phone, open the same link in Chrome for the full-screen feel.
